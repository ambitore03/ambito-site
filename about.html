About Ámbito

Real estate built around how you actually want to live.

Licensed real estate agent based in San Juan, Puerto Rico, representing buyers and sellers with a private, curated approach.

Licensed Real Estate Agent, PR San Juan Board of Realtors

I started Ámbito Real Estate because I kept watching clients stumble with the same problems: too many listings, not good communication, over promise and under deliver circumstances, overwhelming procedures and unstructured guidance.

As a licensed professional and member of the San Juan Board of Realtors, I built my practice around a simple idea: discover what is the end goal of the client, treat it as if it was our own mission and make any Real Estate transaction as easy as possible.

Whether you're buying your first home, relocating, or selling a property you've owned for years, my job is to bring local knowledge, a clear process, and honest guidance to deposit confidence in every step — from the first conversation and forever.
